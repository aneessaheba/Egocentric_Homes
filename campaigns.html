About this campaign

We're building the world's largest open egocentric household dataset. This campaign focuses on kitchen activities — the single most common room for hand-object interaction. Your clips go directly into our annotation pipeline and come back fully labelled with hand pose, segmentation masks, depth maps, and narration.

How to participate

1

Register or sign in

Create a free account and join this campaign from your dashboard.

2

Record your clips

Use a GoPro, phone, or any wearable camera. Clip must be 30–120 seconds, first-person only, well-lit kitchen setting.

3

Submit your footage

Upload your .mp4 file. Our pipeline scores every submission automatically — resolution, hand visibility, sharpness, and narration.

4

Win

Top-scoring submission takes ₹50,000. Runner-up takes ₹25,000. Prizes paid within 7 days of campaign close.

Rules

  • First-person (egocentric) footage only — no third-person angles.
  • Kitchen setting only. Hands must be visible for at least 80% of the clip.
  • Minimum 1080p resolution at 29.9 FPS or higher.
  • Narrate what you're doing in real time — audio is scored.
  • One submission per person. You may revise before the deadline.
  • No scripted, staged, or AI-generated footage.
  • No footage recorded in commercial or restaurant kitchens.
